## ✨ Features

- 🎨 Modern, colorful UI design

- 📋 Category tabs (Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks)
- 🛒 Shopping cart with real-time total
- ⚡ Today's special deals
- 📱 Fully responsive mobile-first design
- 💾 Offline support with Service Worker
- 📲 Installable as mobile app (PWA)

## 📲 How to Install on Your Phone

### For Android:

1. Open Chrome browser on your Android phone
2. Visit: https://mash2006.github.io/college-canteen-ux-demo/
3. Tap the menu (⋮) in the top right
4. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
5. Confirm installation
6. The app icon will appear on your home screen!

### For iPhone (iOS):

1. Open Safari browser on your iPhone
2. Visit: https://mash2006.github.io/college-canteen-ux-demo/
3. Tap the Share button (📤)
4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Tap "Add" in the top right
6. The app icon will appear on your home screen!
